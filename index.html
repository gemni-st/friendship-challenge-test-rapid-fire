This is the full test: thirty questions in three rounds of ten, designed to be answered fast. Read each question aloud, give the player five seconds, and move on. No second chances, no partial credit.

Round One: The Basics

  1. What is my full name, middle name included?
  2. What month was I born?
  3. What is my favorite color?
  4. Tea or coffee, and how do I take it?
  5. What is my go to comfort meal?
  6. What is my shoe size?
  7. What is my most used emoji?
  8. What is my phone wallpaper right now?
  9. What is my go to karaoke song?
  10. What time do I usually wake up?

Round Two: Memories

  1. Where did we first meet?
  2. What was I wearing, as far as you remember?
  3. What is the funniest thing that ever happened to us?
  4. What trip do I still talk about?
  5. What gift from me did you like most?
  6. What is my most embarrassing moment you witnessed?
  7. What inside joke do we reference most?
  8. What was our first big disagreement about?
  9. What habit of mine do you tease me for?
  10. What moment made us close friends?

Round Three: Deep Cuts

  1. What am I most afraid of?
  2. What is my biggest dream right now?
  3. What is the compliment I never forget?
  4. What do I complain about most?
  5. What is my love language, honestly?
  6. What would I do with a free month and no responsibilities?
  7. What is the hardest thing I have been through?
  8. What do I want people to remember about me?
  9. What is something I have never told most people?
  10. What does our friendship mean to me? Trick question, only you know if they get this one.

Scoring Your Friendship Challenge Test

Tally the results out of thirty. Twenty five and up means you are practically telepathic. Fifteen to twenty four is a strong, honest friendship with room to grow. Below fifteen simply means you have wonderful conversations ahead of you. Run this friendship challenge test style quiz twice a year and watch the score climb as your friendship deepens.
